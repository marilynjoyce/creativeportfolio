# mareomni — Marilyn

A personal portfolio with an illustrated lofi bedroom, four short films, a NYC café promo, Substack writing, and a connected map of creative interests. Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; no build step.

## Preview

Serve this folder with a static web server, for example `python3 -m http.server 4173`, then visit http://localhost:4173.

## Editing

- `catalogue.js`: the curated collection. Every piece has one primary territory, multiple connected territories, two to five topic tags, one medium, source links, and optional tone and native video.
- `app.js`: TV channels, video players, article previews, topic connections, search, medium filters, and discovery controls.
- `index.html`: room, sections, and social links.
- `style.css`: responsive layout, map, and room styles.
- `media/`: the original AI-generated room illustration and browser-compatible MP4 copies of the five owner-supplied MOV files. Originals remain untouched.

## Collection

The collection has 38 pieces: four native short films, the café promo, selected Instagram commentary and music, 12 additional TikTok videos, and six Substack articles. Duplicate cross-posts, the Owl City video, and unrelated beauty/trend posts are excluded. Every video has a local cover image shared by its map preview and collection entry. The TikTok additions were selected from visible public captions. Working titles and tags are editorial, with the CS and AI-art film meanings corrected from the owner's descriptions.

Six territories organize the map: life & philosophy; technology & society; ambition & psychology; art & creativity; music & sound; play & culture. Eighteen topic nodes connect through shared pieces. Territory and topic selections filter the collection; format and text search can be combined. The map also supports keyboard focus and touch selection. Surprise me opens a random matching piece.

The TV defaults to a muted loop of “I thought it would feel different,” with a pause button and four film channels. Clicking a film opens a native video player with sound controls. Reduced-motion preferences disable automatic playback. TikTok-only work uses official TikTok embeds; other Instagram work uses Instagram embeds, with original links for both. Substack writing opens the original article.

## Room and writing presentation

On phones the illustrated room fills the viewport, with the TV aligned to its artwork and social links at the bottom. All interface arrows are lightweight SVGs to avoid platform emoji rendering. Writing reuses the six published entries as cream paper previews, with a featured essay and restrained desktop overlap; mobile uses a readable vertical stack. Paper transitions respect reduced motion. The About section uses the owner’s supplied copy.
